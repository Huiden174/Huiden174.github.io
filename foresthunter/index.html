Forest Hunter 林境猎人 — Privacy Policy

Last updated / 最后更新：24 November 2026

English · 中文

This policy explains how Forest Hunter (林境猎人) (the "App") handles information. The App is developed by Huiden Chen, an independent developer based in Malaysia.

1. Information stored on your device

You do not need an account to play. Your game progress, items, in-game currencies, purchase records needed to deliver what you bought, and settings are saved on your device. We do not run servers and do not receive your save data. The "Player ID" shown in Settings is a random string created on your device; it contains no personal information and is only used if you contact us about your save.

We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photos, or microphone data. The App uses GameAnalytics to understand how the game is played (see section 2b).

2. Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the App backs up your save (the data described in section 1) to Google Play Games "Saved Games", linked to your Play Games account, so you can continue on another device or after reinstalling. The App receives your Play Games sign-in status and uses Google's storage for that one save slot; we do not receive your Google account details. You can use the App without signing in, and you can delete Play Games data for the App from your Google account (Play Games settings). Google's handling is described in the Google privacy policy.

2a. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App offers optional rewarded ads (for example, to recover items after a failed expedition). Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one. The App uses Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may process your IP address (including approximate location derived from it), the advertising ID and other device identifiers, device information, and ad interactions.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → 广告隐私选项 (Ad privacy options). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2b. Gameplay analytics (GameAnalytics)

The App uses GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark) to understand how players progress and where the game is too hard or too easy. The App sends: when an expedition starts, ends or fails and in which area, the number of animals defeated, level-ups, and actions in the camp such as research bought or gear upgraded; plus device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language, country derived from the IP address) and error reports.

The App uses a random identifier created for analytics; it does not send the advertising ID, your name, email or Google account. Data is sent over an encrypted connection and processed by GameAnalytics on our behalf; we use it only to improve the game and do not sell it. GameAnalytics privacy policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

2c. Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the App submits your total hunter XP and the furthest forest area you reached to Google Play Games leaderboards. Your Play Games profile name and avatar may be shown to other players on these leaderboards according to your Play Games profile visibility settings. You can change these settings or delete Play Games data for the App in your Google account.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Some items can be bought with real money through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed entirely by Google; we never see your card or payment details. The App only receives the purchase result, the product ID and a purchase token, which it stores on your device so that each purchase is delivered exactly once. Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Payments privacy notice: Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Crash and performance data

Google Play may collect crash and performance information about the App (Android vitals) under Google's own policies and your device settings. We use this information only to fix problems.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is processed by Google and GameAnalytics only as described above to provide ads, payments, gameplay analytics, leaderboards and app diagnostics, or where required by law. If you email us, we receive your email address and message so that we can reply.

6. Retention and deletion

Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage deletes the save data on your device. If you use the cloud save, a copy remains in your Google Play Games account until you delete it there; without the cloud save, the deletion cannot be undone and purchased items stored in that save are removed with it. Information processed by Google is kept and deleted according to Google's policies and your Google account and device settings. You can contact us with any request about information related to you.

7. Children

The App is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us using the email below and we will respond under applicable law.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The latest version is always posted on this page with the date at the top.

本政策说明 林境猎人（Forest Hunter）（以下简称"本游戏"）如何处理信息。本游戏由马来西亚的独立开发者 Huiden Chen 开发。

1. 保存在你设备上的信息

游戏无需注册账号。你的游戏进度、物品、游戏内货币、用来发放已购商品的购买记录，以及各项设置，保存在你的设备上。我们没有服务器，不会收到你的存档。设置页显示的「玩家编号」是在你设备上随机生成的字符串，不包含任何个人信息，只在你就存档问题联系我们时使用。

我们不收集你的姓名、通讯录、精确位置、照片或麦克风数据。本游戏使用 GameAnalytics 了解游戏的玩法数据（见第 2b 节）。

2. 云存档（Google Play 游戏）

如果你在设备上登录了 Google Play 游戏，本游戏会把第 1 节所述的存档备份到与你 Play 游戏账号关联的「已保存的游戏」，方便你换手机或重装后继续游玩。本游戏只获取你的 Play 游戏登录状态，并使用 Google 提供的这一个存档位，我们收不到你的 Google 账号信息。不登录也可以正常游戏；你可以在 Google 账号的 Play 游戏设置中删除本游戏的数据。

2a. 广告（Google AdMob）

本游戏提供可选的激励广告（例如在远征失败后找回物品），只有在你主动选择观看时才会播放。本游戏使用 Google AdMob。为了投放和统计广告、防止作弊，Google 可能会处理你的 IP 地址（包括由此推断的大致位置）、广告标识符及其他设备标识符、设备信息和广告互动数据。

在欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士，本游戏会先通过 Google 的同意弹窗征求你的同意，之后才会请求广告。在适用的地区，你可以随时在游戏内的「设置 → 广告隐私选项」中更改选择。你也可以在 Android 系统设置中重置或删除广告标识符。

Google 隐私权政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy

2b. 游戏数据分析（GameAnalytics）

本游戏使用 GameAnalytics（丹麦 GameAnalytics ApS）了解玩家的进度，以及哪里太难或太容易。本游戏会发送：远征的开始、完成或失败以及所在林区、击倒的动物数量、升级，以及营地中的操作（例如购买研究、升级装备）；还有设备与应用信息（设备型号、系统版本、应用版本、语言、由 IP 地址推断的国家）和错误报告。

本游戏使用为数据分析专门生成的随机标识符，不会发送广告标识符、你的姓名、邮箱或 Google 账号。数据通过加密连接传输，由 GameAnalytics 代表我们处理；我们只用它来改进游戏，不会出售。GameAnalytics 隐私政策：https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

2c. 排行榜（Google Play 游戏）

如果你登录了 Google Play 游戏，本游戏会把你的猎人总经验和到达的最远林区提交到 Google Play 游戏排行榜。根据你在 Play 游戏中的个人资料可见性设置，其他玩家可能会在排行榜上看到你的 Play 游戏昵称和头像。你可以在 Google 账号中更改这些设置，或删除本游戏的 Play 游戏数据。

3. 应用内购买（Google Play）

部分物品可以通过 Google Play 结算用真实货币购买。付款全部由 Google 处理，我们看不到你的银行卡或付款信息。本游戏只会收到购买结果、商品编号和购买凭据，并把它们保存在你的设备上，用于确保每笔购买只发放一次。

4. 崩溃与性能数据

Google Play 可能会按照 Google 自己的政策和你的设备设置，收集本游戏的崩溃和性能信息（Android vitals）。我们只用这些信息修复问题。

5. 共享

我们不会出售你的个人信息。除法律要求外，信息仅由 Google 和 GameAnalytics 按上文所述处理，用于广告、付款、游戏数据分析、排行榜和应用诊断。如果你发邮件给我们，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件内容，用于回复你。

6. 保留与删除

卸载本游戏或清除其存储空间，会删除设备上的存档。如果你使用了云存档，Play 游戏账号中的副本会一直保留，直到你在那里删除；没有使用云存档时，删除后无法恢复，存档中已购买的物品也会一并删除。Google 处理的信息按照 Google 的政策以及你的 Google 账号和设备设置保留和删除。关于与你有关的信息，你可以随时联系我们。

7. 儿童

本游戏面向 13 岁及以上的玩家，不面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。如果你认为有儿童通过本游戏提供了个人信息，请联系我们。

8. 你的权利

根据你所在地区的法律，你可能有权访问、更正或删除个人信息，或反对某些处理。请通过下方邮箱联系我们，我们会依法回复。
