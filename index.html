Time Bunny Bandits — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Time Bunny Bandits (package com.huidenchen.timebunnybandits, "the App") handles information. The App is developed by an independent developer based in Malaysia ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location, and we do not require an account to play.

  • Game progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, heroes, upgrades, stamina and settings) is stored only on your device.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase confirmation from Google so we can deliver the item; we never see your payment card details.
  • Gameplay statistics (for example which level you played, whether you won, how many stars you earned or which wave you reached, which hero you used, upgrades, coins and gems earned and spent, ads watched and items bought), when you open and close the App and how long you play, and error reports (the text of a script error or crash, with no personal data) are sent to GameAnalytics with a random ID created when the App is installed, your device model and Android version. GameAnalytics also receives your IP address as part of the connection. We use this data only to balance the game and fix bugs, and we do not sell it. The random ID is not linked to your name, Google account or advertising ID.

2. Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional reward videos (for example for extra stamina, free gems or double coins) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device information and ad interaction data. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Players in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time from the App's settings. Everyone can reset or delete their advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in the Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads). Players who buy "Remove Ads" no longer see the ads between levels.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play Services / Google Play Billing — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • GameAnalytics (gameplay statistics) — GameAnalytics Privacy Policy

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device; uninstalling the App deletes it. To ask about data held by Google for ads, see the Google links above. Gameplay statistics are kept by GameAnalytics under its own retention policy; to have the statistics for your install ID deleted, contact us.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 or the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access or delete your personal data. Contact us using the details below.
