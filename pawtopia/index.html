# Pawtopia — Privacy Policy Last updated: 30 October 2026 This policy explains how **Pawtopia** (Android package `com.huidenchen.pawtopia`, the App) handles information. The App is developed by **Huiden Chen**, an independent developer based in Malaysia. ## 1. Information handled by the App You do not need an account to play. Your nickname, guest profile, game progress, currencies, unlocked levels and settings are saved locally on your device. The App does not send your nickname or guest profile to GameAnalytics, and the current version does not provide online account binding or cloud saves. ## 2. Gameplay analytics and diagnostics We use **GameAnalytics** to understand game difficulty and improve reliability. The App sends a randomly generated installation identifier, session identifiers and session counts; device model, operating system and app version; session duration; difficulty and level played; level completion, timeout or exit; elapsed time, puppies found and stars; successful item use and virtual-currency transactions; and successful real-ad engagement when applicable. Limited diagnostic reports contain code file names, line numbers and function names, rather than raw error messages or player-entered text. An interrupted session may be recorded without being classified as a confirmed crash. GameAnalytics receives your IP address when the App connects to its servers. Our integration does not supply your name, email address, nickname, Google account, Android ID or advertising ID to GameAnalytics. Its installation identifier is pseudonymous, not a guarantee of anonymity. We use these statistics to balance levels, understand feature usage and investigate technical issues. Events may be temporarily stored on your device while offline and sent later over HTTPS. The local event queue is limited to 500 events. The installation identifier remains until app data is cleared or the App is uninstalled. GameAnalytics privacy information: https://www.gameanalytics.com/privacy ## 3. Advertising The App integrates **Google AdMob** for interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Depending on configuration and your choices, Google's advertising services may process IP addresses (including approximate location derived from them), device or advertising identifiers, device information, app/ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. The current release uses Google test ad units; this does not mean the advertising service has no data processing. You can manage or delete your advertising ID using your Android device's privacy or ads settings. Advertising choices and consent options may depend on your location and the advertising service's configuration. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads ## 4. Purchases The current version uses a clearly labelled simulated purchase flow. It does not charge real money or collect payment card details. Spending in-game coins or gems may generate gameplay analytics as described above. If real-money purchases are introduced, we will update this policy to describe the actual payment processing. ## 5. Contact messages and sharing If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message to respond to your request. We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared with the services described in this policy to provide advertising, gameplay analytics and diagnostics, or where required by law. ## 6. Retention and deletion Local game data can be removed by clearing the App's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the App. Removing local data does not automatically delete information previously received by Google or GameAnalytics. Analytics and advertising data are retained according to the relevant provider's retention policies and applicable settings. Contact us to request access or deletion of information we can identify and control. We may need additional information, such as the analytics installation identifier, to locate pseudonymous records. For information held independently by Google, use Google's privacy controls or request channels. ## 7. Children The App is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us. ## 8. Your choices and rights Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to certain processing of your personal information. Contact us using the email below. We will assess and respond to requests under applicable law. ## 9. Changes We may update this policy as the App changes.
